A garden table, a banquet hall, and a menu that doesn't rush you

Hotel Kanha Resort & Restaurant sits opposite the Indian Oil petrol pump on the Neemuch–Mhow Road in Ghatabillod, just outside Pithampur. What started as a roadside family restaurant in 2009 has grown into a full garden dining destination — multi-cuisine menu, an open-air garden, a kids' play area, and a banquet hall that hosts everything from birthdays to wedding functions.

No rooms yet — this is a dining and events address, not a stay. Everything on the menu is vegetarian.
